CAN SNAP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Contact: cansnap.help@gmail.com. Developer: Noam Firuz, Israel.

1. Short version. You can play without an account. We never ask for your email, phone, location, contacts or photos. Nothing is sold. Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a reward.

2. On your phone. Your progress (coins, cans, settings) is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Online features (leagues, friends, trades, clubs, Snap Runs, alerts). When you use them, the game creates a random player id and stores, on our server: that id, the nickname and club name you choose, your scores and game items, your friends list, and (only if you turn alerts on) a push subscription. Your nickname and club name are visible to other players. Our server runs on Cloudflare, which processes IP addresses to deliver traffic.

4. Rewarded ads. If you tap a "watch an ad" button, Google AdMob shows a video ad. AdMob (Google) may use your device's advertising identifier and basic device data to show and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). On iPhone you are asked first whether to allow tracking; saying no still gives every reward. Players who buy the Supporter pack never see ads.

5. Purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check them; RevenueCat receives an anonymous id and the store receipt, never your card details.

6. Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Public names are filtered.

7. Keeping and deleting data. Server data is kept while you play and deleted 12 months after your last online visit. Email cansnap.help@gmail.com with your friend code to delete it sooner.

8. Changes. We will update this page and the date above.
